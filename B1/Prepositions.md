# B1 Grammar: Prepositions (Präpositionen)

At the B1 level, you learn prepositions that dictate the **Genitive** case. These prepositions are highly common in written German, news articles, and formal conversations.

---

## Genitive Prepositions

The four most common Genitive prepositions are:

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **während** | during | **Während des** Fluges darf man nicht rauchen. (During the flight, smoking is not allowed. - *Masc. Gen: des Fluges*) |
| **wegen** | because of | **Wegen der** Kälte bleiben wir im Haus. (Because of the cold, we stay in the house. - *Fem. Gen: der Kälte*) |
| **trotz** | despite / in spite of | **Trotz des** Regens gehen wir spazieren. (Despite the rain, we go for a walk. - *Masc. Gen: des Regens*) |
| **(an)statt** | instead of | Ich nehme die Treppe **statt des** Aufzugs. (I take the stairs instead of the elevator. - *Masc. Gen: des Aufzugs*) |

---

## Additional Genitive Prepositions (B1+)

As you progress through B1, you will also encounter:

- **außerhalb** (outside of):
  - Er wohnt **außerhalb der** Stadt. (He lives outside of the city.)
- **innerhalb** (inside of / within):
  - Bitte antworten Sie **innerhalb einer** Woche. (Please answer within a week.)
- **mithilfe** (with the help of):
  - Er hat das Regal **mithilfe seines** Vaters gebaut. (He built the shelf with the help of his father.)

---

## Spoken German Exception
In daily spoken German, many native speakers use the **Dative** case after these prepositions (especially *wegen* and *trotz*). While acceptable in casual speech, you must use the **Genitive** in examinations and writing.

- *Formal/Written*: wegen **des** Wetters (Genitive)
- *Informal/Spoken*: wegen **dem** Wetter (Dative)
