# B1 Grammar: Adjectives (Adjektive)

At the B1 level, you must master the remaining two declension patterns for adjectives: **Mixed Declension** (after indefinite articles and possessives) and **Strong Declension** (when no article is present).

---

## 1. Mixed Declension (After *ein, kein, mein...*)

This pattern is used when the noun is preceded by an indefinite article (*ein, eine*), a negative article (*kein, keine*), or a possessive determiner (*mein, dein, sein, ihr, unser, euer, Ihr*).

The adjective takes the ending to signal the gender of the noun in the Nominative and Accusative singular, and defaults to `-en` in all other cases.

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Nominative** | **-er** | -e | **-es** | -en |
| **Accusative** | -en | -e | **-es** | -en |
| **Dative** | -en | -en | -en | -en |
| **Genitive** | -en | -en | -en | -en |

### Examples:
- **Nominative Masculine**: Das ist ein alt**er** Stuhl. (That is an old chair.)
- **Nominative Neuter**: Hier steht ein neu**es** Haus. (Here stands a new house.)
- **Accusative Neuter**: Ich kaufe ein neu**es** Auto. (I am buying a new car.)
- **Dative Plural**: Ich spiele mit meinen klein**en** Kindern. (I play with my small children.)

---

## 2. Strong Declension (No Preceding Article)

This pattern is used when there is **no article** before the adjective. Since there is no article to show the gender or case, the adjective itself must carry the strong signal ending (similar to definite articles).

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Nominative** | -er | -e | -es | -e |
| **Accusative** | -en | -e | -es | -e |
| **Dative** | **-em** | **-er** | **-em** | -en |
| **Genitive** | **-en** | **-er** | **-en** | **-er** |

### Examples:
- **Nominative Masculine**: Kalt**er** Kaffee schmeckt nicht. (Cold coffee doesn't taste good.)
- **Nominative Plural**: Nett**e** Menschen sind hier. (Nice people are here.)
- **Dative Masculine**: Ich trinke Tee aus heiß**em** Glas. (I drink tea out of a hot glass.)
- **Genitive Plural**: Die Beratung groß**er** Firmen ist teuer. (The consulting of big firms is expensive.)
