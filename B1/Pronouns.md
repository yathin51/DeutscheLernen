# B1 Grammar: Pronouns (Pronomen)

At the B1 level, you learn how to connect sentences and add descriptive details using **Relative Pronouns** (*Relativpronomen*) to build **Relative Clauses** (*Relativsätze*).

---

## 1. Relative Pronouns Chart

Relative pronouns look very similar to definite articles (*der, die, das*), with a few exceptions in the Dative plural and Genitive cases.

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Nominative** | der | die | das | die |
| **Accusative** | den | die | das | die |
| **Dative** | dem | der | dem | **denen** |
| **Genitive** | **dessen** | **deren** | **dessen** | **deren** |

---

## 2. Rules for Building Relative Clauses

A relative clause is a subordinate clause (*Nebensatz*). This means:
1. It is separated from the main clause by a **comma**.
2. The **relative pronoun** starts the clause.
3. The **conjugated verb** is pushed to the **very end** of the relative clause.

### How to choose the gender and number?
The relative pronoun's **gender and number** are determined by the noun it refers to in the main clause.
- *Example*: Das ist der **Mann** (masculine, singular).

### How to choose the case?
The relative pronoun's **case** is determined by its grammatical function *inside the relative clause*.

- **Nominative Subject**:
  - Das ist der Mann, **der** (Masc. Nom.) hier wohnt. (That is the man *who* lives here.)
- **Accusative Direct Object**:
  - Das ist der Mann, **den** (Masc. Acc.) ich gestern getroffen habe. (That is the man *whom* I met yesterday.)
- **Dative Indirect Object or after Dative Preposition**:
  - Das ist der Mann, **dem** (Masc. Dat.) ich geholfen habe. (That is the man *whom* I helped.)
  - Das ist der Mann, mit **dem** (Dat. after *mit*) ich gesprochen habe. (That is the man with *whom* I spoke.)
- **Genitive Possession**:
  - Das ist der Mann, **dessen** (Masc. Gen.) Auto kaputt ist. (That is the man *whose* car is broken.)
