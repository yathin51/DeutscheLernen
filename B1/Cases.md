# B1 Grammar: Cases (Kasus)

At the B1 level, you learn the fourth and final German case: the **Genitive Case** (*Genitiv*). The Genitive case is used to show possession, ownership, or relationship (equivalent to the English "of" or apostrophe "'s").

---

## 1. Genitive (Possession / *Wessen-Fall*)
The Genitive case answers the question of ownership.
- **Question**: *Wessen?* (Whose?)
- **Example**: Das ist das Auto **des Vaters**. (That is the car *of the father* / *the father's* car.) -> *Wessen Auto ist das? Des Vaters.*

---

## 2. Article & Noun Declension in Genitive

Pay close attention to how the articles change, and notice that **masculine and neuter singular nouns also receive a suffix ending** (`-s` or `-es`).

| Gender | Definite Article | Indefinite Article | Noun Ending Suffix | Example |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Masculine** | **des** | **eines** | **-(e)s** | des Vater**s** / eines Mann**es** |
| **Feminine** | **der** | **einer** | - | der Frau / einer Mutter |
| **Neuter** | **des** | **eines** | **-(e)s** | des Kind**es** / eines Auto**s** |
| **Plural** | **der** | **keiner** | - | der Kinder / keiner Freunde |

### Rule for Noun Suffixes (`-s` vs. `-es`):
- Add **`-s`** to multi-syllable nouns or nouns ending in a vowel: *das Auto* -> *des Auto**s***, *der König* -> *des König**s***.
- Add **`-es`** to one-syllable nouns or nouns ending in `s`, `ß`, `x`, `z`, `tz`: *der Mann* -> *des Mann**es***, *das Haus* -> *des Haus**es***, *der Fluss* -> *des Fluss**es***.

---

## 3. Substitutes for Genitive in Spoken German
In informal spoken German, native speakers often replace the Genitive case with the preposition **von** + **Dative**.

- **Genitive (Written/Formal)**: Die Katze **meiner Schwester** schläft. (My sister's cat sleeps.)
- **Dative Substitute (Spoken/Informal)**: Die Katze **von meiner Schwester** schläft.
- **Genitive (Written/Formal)**: Das Spielzeug **des Kindes** ist hier. (The child's toy is here.)
- **Dative Substitute (Spoken/Informal)**: Das Spielzeug **von dem Kind** ist hier.
