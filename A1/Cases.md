# A1 Grammar: Cases (Kasus)

In German, nouns and pronouns play different roles in a sentence. These roles are determined by **cases**. At the A1 level, we focus on the first two cases: **Nominative** and **Accusative**.

---

## 1. Nominative (Subject / *Wer-Fall*)
The Nominative case is the subject of the sentence—the person or thing performing the action.
- **Question**: *Wer?* (Who?) or *Was?* (What?)
- **Example**: **Der Vater** schläft. (The father is sleeping.) -> *Wer schläft? Der Vater.*

### Nominative Articles
| Gender | Definite (The) | Indefinite (A / An) | Negative (No) | Possessive (My) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Masculine** | der | ein | kein | mein |
| **Feminine** | die | eine | keine | meine |
| **Neuter** | das | ein | kein | mein |
| **Plural** | die | - | keine | meine |

---

## 2. Accusative (Direct Object / *Wen-Fall*)
The Accusative case is the direct object—the person or thing receiving the action directly.
- **Question**: *Wen?* (Whom?) or *Was?* (What?)
- **Example**: Ich habe **einen Hund**. (I have a dog.) -> *Was habe ich? Einen Hund.*

### Accusative Articles (Only Masculine Changes!)
Notice that only the masculine article changes (to `-en`). Feminine, neuter, and plural stay exactly the same as in the Nominative case.

| Gender | Definite (The) | Indefinite (A / An) | Negative (No) | Possessive (My) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Masculine** | **den** | **einen** | **keinen** | **meinen** |
| **Feminine** | die | eine | keine | meine |
| **Neuter** | das | ein | kein | mein |
| **Plural** | die | - | keine | meine |

---

## 3. Accusative-Triggering Verbs (A1)
Certain verbs always require the direct object to be in the Accusative case:
- **haben** (to have): Ich habe **einen** Bruder. (I have a brother.)
- **brauchen** (to need): Sie braucht **ein** Auto. (She needs a car.)
- **sehen** (to see): Er sieht **den** Mann. (He sees the man.)
- **mögen** (to like): Wir mögen **die** Lehrerin. (We like the teacher.)
- **kaufen** (to buy): Ich kaufe **keinen** Kaffee. (I am not buying coffee.)
- **essen/trinken** (to eat/drink): Ich esse **einen** Apfel. (I eat an apple.)
