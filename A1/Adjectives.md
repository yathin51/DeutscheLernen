# A1 Grammar: Adjectives (Adjektive)

At the A1 level, adjectives are used in their simplest form: after the verb *sein* (to be) or *werden* (to become). 

When adjectives are placed **after the verb** (predicative use), they **do not change their ending**!

---

## 1. Predicative Use (No Endings)
- *der Mann* (masculine): Der Mann ist **nett**. (The man is nice.)
- *die Frau* (feminine): Die Frau ist **nett**. (The woman is nice.)
- *das Auto* (neuter): Das Auto ist **schnell**. (The car is fast.)
- *die Autos* (plural): Die Autos sind **schnell**. (The cars are fast.)

---

## 2. Essential A1 Adjectives & Antonyms

Learning adjectives in pairs of opposites is an effective way to build vocabulary.

| Adjective | Meaning | Opposing Adjective | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **groß** | big / tall | **klein** | small / short |
| **gut** | good | **schlecht** | bad |
| **schön** | beautiful | **hässlich** | ugly |
| **alt** | old | **jung / neu** | young / new |
| **schnell** | fast | **langsam** | slow |
| **kalt** | cold | **heiß** | hot |
| **teuer** | expensive | **billig / günstig** | cheap / affordable |
| **glücklich** | happy | **traurig** | sad |
| **einfach** | easy / simple | **schwer / schwierig** | difficult |
| **laut** | loud | **leise** | quiet |
