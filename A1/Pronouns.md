# A1 Grammar: Pronouns (Pronomen)

At the A1 level, you learn how to talk about yourself, address others, and indicate simple possession using pronouns.

---

## 1. Personal Pronouns (Nominative)
These are used as the subject of the sentence to replace nouns.

| Pronoun | English | Meaning / Usage | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ich** | I | 1st person singular | **Ich** wohne in Berlin. |
| **du** | you | 2nd person singular (informal) | Woher kommst **du**? |
| **er** | he / it | 3rd person singular masculine | Das ist der Tisch. **Er** ist alt. |
| **sie** | she / it | 3rd person singular feminine | Das ist die Lampe. **Sie** ist neu. |
| **es** | it | 3rd person singular neuter | Das ist das Buch. **Es** ist gut. |
| **wir** | we | 1st person plural | **Wir** lernen Deutsch. |
| **ihr** | you all | 2nd person plural (informal) | Was macht **ihr** heute? |
| **sie** | they | 3rd person plural | Meine Eltern kommen. **Sie** sind nett. |
| **Sie** | You | Formal singular & plural (always capitalized) | Wie heißen **Sie**? |

---

## 2. Possessive Pronouns (Nominative)
Possessive pronouns indicate ownership (my, your). At A1, we focus on **mein** (my) and **dein** (your - informal).

The ending changes depending on the grammatical gender of the noun that follows it:

| Owner | Masculine (*der*) | Feminine (*die*) | Neuter (*das*) | Plural (*die*) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **ich** (my) | mein | mein**e** | mein | mein**e** |
| **du** (your) | dein | dein**e** | dein | dein**e** |

### Examples:
- Das ist **mein** Vater (masculine).
- Das ist **meine** Mutter (feminine).
- Ist das **dein** Buch (neuter)?
- Wo sind **deine** Schlüssel (plural)?
