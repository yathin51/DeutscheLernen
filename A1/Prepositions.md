# A1 Grammar: Prepositions (Präpositionen)

Prepositions link words together to show location, direction, or time. At A1, we learn the most common prepositions in context.

---

## 1. Local Prepositions (Places & Direction)

### **in** (in / inside)
Used for countries with articles, buildings, and enclosed spaces.
- Ich wohne **in** Berlin. (I live in Berlin.)
- Ich bin **in der** Schule. (I am in school.)

### **aus** (from / out of)
Used for country/city of origin.
- Ich komme **aus** Italien. (I come from Italy.)
- Er geht **aus dem** Zimmer. (He walks out of the room.)

### **nach** (to)
Used for countries (without articles), cities, and the phrase *nach Hause* (to home).
- Ich fliege **nach** Deutschland. (I am flying to Germany.)
- Ich gehe jetzt **nach** Hause. (I am going home now.)

### **zu** (to)
Used for people, specific local destinations, and the phrase *zu Hause* (at home).
- Ich gehe **zu** meinem Freund. (I am going to my friend's house.)
- Ich bin **zu** Hause. (I am at home.)

---

## 2. Temporal Prepositions (Time)

### **um** (at)
Used for exact times of the day.
- Der Kurs beginnt **um** 9:00 Uhr. (The course starts at 9:00.)

### **am** (on / at the)
Contraction of *an dem*. Used for days of the week, times of day (except night), and dates.
- **Am** Montag spiele ich Tennis. (On Monday I play tennis.)
- **Am** Nachmittag lerne ich Deutsch. (In the afternoon I learn German.)

### **im** (in / in the)
Contraction of *in dem*. Used for months and seasons.
- Mein Geburtstag ist **im** Juli. (My birthday is in July.)
- **Im** Winter schneit es. (In winter it snows.)

---

## 3. Other Common Prepositions

### **mit** (with / by)
Used to express doing something with someone, or traveling by vehicle.
- Ich spiele **mit** meinem Kind. (I play with my child.)
- Ich fahre **mit dem** Auto. (I drive by car.)

### **für** (for)
- Das Geschenk ist **für** meine Mutter. (The present is for my mother.)
