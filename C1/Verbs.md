# C1 Grammar: Verbs (Verben) - Subjunctive I (Konjunktiv I)

At the C1 level, you learn how to report statements and speech objectively. In formal German journalism, reports, and literature, this is achieved using **Subjunctive I** (*Konjunktiv I*), also known as indirect speech (*Indirekte Rede*).

---

## 1. Usage of Konjunktiv I
Konjunktiv I is used to show that you are quoting or reporting someone else's statement without taking a stance on its truthfulness. It separates the reporter from the source.

- **Direct Speech**: Der Minister sagt: "Ich **bin** unschuldig." (The minister says: "I am innocent.")
- **Indirect Speech (Konjunktiv I)**: Der Minister sagt, er **sei** unschuldig. (The minister says that he is innocent.)

If you used the regular indicative (*er ist*), it implies that you personally agree it is a fact. Using *er sei* remains neutral.

---

## 2. Conjugation of Konjunktiv I
The stem is taken from the infinitive, and the endings are added:

| Pronoun | Ending | Example: *fragen* | Example: *kommen* |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **ich** | `-e` | ich frage* | ich komme* |
| **du** | `-est` | du fragest | du kommest |
| **er/sie/es** | `-e` | er/sie/es frag**e** | er/sie/es komm**e** |
| **wir** | `-en` | wir fragen* | wir kommen* |
| **ihr** | `-et` | ihr fraget | ihr kommet |
| **sie/Sie** | `-en` | sie/Sie fragen* | sie/Sie kommen* |

### *The "Identity Conflict" Rule:
Whenever the Konjunktiv I form is **identical** to the indicative present tense form (which happens for *ich*, *wir*, and *sie/Sie*), you must replace it with **Konjunktiv II** (or *würden* + infinitive) to ensure the subjunctive meaning is clear.

- **Direct**: Sie sagen: "Wir **haben** kein Geld."
- **Indirect**: Sie sagen, sie **haben** (Identical to present) -> Replace with Konjunktiv II: Sie sagen, sie **hätten** kein Geld.

---

## 3. The Irregular Verb *sein*
The verb *sein* is completely irregular in Konjunktiv I and never uses Konjunktiv II substitutes because it is always distinct.

| Pronoun | Konjunktiv I Form | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ich** | sei | ... er sagte, ich **sei** hier. |
| **du** | seiest / seist | ... er sagte, du **seiest** hier. |
| **er/sie/es** | **sei** | ... er sagte, er **sei** hier. |
| **wir** | seien | ... er sagte, wir **seien** hier. |
| **ihr** | seiet | ... er sagte, ihr **seiet** hier. |
| **sie/Sie** | seien | ... er sagte, sie **seien** hier. |
