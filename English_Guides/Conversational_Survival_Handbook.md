# 🗣️ German Conversational Survival Handbook: Real-World Scenarios

> *"In real life, Germans appreciate direct, polite, and precise language. This handbook covers the exact phrases, responses, and social scripts you need from stepping off the plane to ordering in restaurants and managing emergencies."*

---

## 🧭 The Golden Civility Rule: Formal (*Sie*) vs. Informal (*du*)

In Germany, Austria, and Switzerland:
- Use **Sie** (verb ending in *-en*) with adults you do not know, store clerks, officials, doctors, professors, and business partners.
- Use **du** with friends, family, children under 16, fellow university students, and in casual creative/startup workplaces.

## ☕ 1. Greetings, Small Talk & Courtesies

### Essential Greetings & Farewells
| English | Authentic German | Pronunciation Guide | Social Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hello / Good day | **Guten Tag!** | *GOO-ten TAHK* | Standard polite daytime greeting everywhere. |
| Good morning | **Guten Morgen!** | *GOO-ten MOR-gen* | Used until around 10:30–11:00 AM. |
| Good evening | **Guten Abend!** | *GOO-ten AH-bent* | Used from ~6:00 PM onwards. |
| Hi / Casual hello | **Hallo!** | *HAH-lo* | Universally friendly among all age groups. |
| Regional Hello (South/Austria) | **Grüß Gott! / Servus!** | *grewss GOT / ZAIR-voos* | Bavaria, Baden-Württemberg, and Austria. |
| Regional Hello (North) | **Moin! / Moin Moin!** | *MOYN* | Hamburg, Bremen, Northern Germany (all day). |
| Goodbye (Formal) | **Auf Wiedersehen!** | *owf VEE-der-zay-en* | Standard when parting in shops, offices, calls. |
| Bye (Casual) | **Tschüss! / Ciao!** | *CHUSS / CHOW* | Standard everyday friendly goodbye. |
| Have a nice day! | **Schönen Tag noch!** | *SHUR-nen TAHK nokh* | Standard farewell heard at supermarket checkouts. |
| Same to you! | **Danke, gleichfalls!** | *DAHN-kuh GLYKH-fahls* | Polite automatic response to any wish. |

### Small Talk & Getting to Know People
| English Question | German Phrase | Authentic Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| How are you? (Formal) | **Wie geht es Ihnen?** | *Sehr gut, danke. Und Ihnen?* (Very well, thanks. And you?) |
| How are you? (Casual) | **Wie geht's? / Alles gut?** | *Passt schon! / Ganz gut, danke!* (Going fine! / Pretty good!) |
| What's your name? (Formal) | **Wie heißen Sie?** | *Ich heiße David Smith. / Mein Name ist...* |
| What's your name? (Casual) | **Wie heißt du?** | *Ich bin Sarah.* |
| Where are you from? | **Woher kommen Sie?** | *Ich komme aus Kanada / den USA / Indien.* |
| Where do you live? | **Wo wohnen Sie?** | *Ich wohne jetzt in Frankfurt.* |
| What do you do for a living? | **Was machen Sie beruflich?** | *Ich arbeite als Software-Entwickler.* |
| Do you speak English? | **Sprechen Sie Englisch?** | *Ein bisschen. / Ja, fließend.* (A bit / Yes, fluently.) |

| What does that mean? | **Was bedeutet das?** | *Das bedeutet...* |

## 🛒 2. Shopping, Supermarket & Numbers

Supermarket checkouts (*die Kasse*) in Germany are famously fast! Know these phrases:

### Supermarket Speed Script
1. Cashier scans items and asks:  
   **"Brauchen Sie einen Kassenzettel / Bon?"** *(Do you need a receipt?)*  
   ➔ You reply: *"Nein, danke!"* or *"Ja, bitte."*
2. Cashier asks:  
   **"Haben Sie eine Payback-Karte?"** *(Do you have a rewards card?)*  
   ➔ You reply: *"Nein, habe ich nicht."*
3. Cashier announces the total:  
   **"Das macht 18 Euro 45, bitte."** *(That comes to €18.45, please.)*
4. Payment:  
   - Paying by card: **"Mit Karte, bitte."**
   - Paying with cash: **"Bar, bitte."**
   - Keep the change: **"Stimmt so!"** *(Never say "Behalten Sie das Wechselgeld!")*

### Practical Shopping Vocabulary & Requests
| English | German | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Where can I find milk? | **Wo finde ich bitte die Milch?** | Always add *bitte*! |
| How much does this cost? | **Was kostet das? / Wie viel kostet das?** | Simple and direct. |
| Is this on sale? | **Ist das im Angebot?** | *Angebot* = special offer. |
| Do you have a bag? | **Haben Sie eine Tüte für mich?** | Plastic bags cost ~15–30 cents. |
| The deposit system | **das Pfand / die Pfandflasche** | Return bottles to the *Pfandautomat* for credit! |

## 🚆 3. Getting Around & Public Transport (ÖPNV)

| English | German | Example / Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Excuse me, where is the station? | **Entschuldigung, wo ist der Bahnhof?** | *Gleich um die Ecke.* (Right around the corner.) |
| How do I get to the city center? | **Wie komme ich zum Stadtzentrum?** | *Nehmen Sie die U-Bahn-Linie 2.* |
| A single ticket to Berlin, please. | **Eine einfache Fahrt nach Berlin, bitte.** | *Einfache Fahrt* = one-way ticket. |
| Round trip | **Hin- und Rückfahrt** | Return ticket. |
| Which track does the train leave from? | **Von welchem Gleis fährt der Zug ab?** | *Gleis 4, Abschnitt C.* |
| Does this bus go to the airport? | **Fährt dieser Bus zum Flughafen?** | *Ja, Endstation Flughafen Terminal 1.* |
| Delay / Cancelled | **die Verspätung / fällt aus** | *"Der ICE hat ca. 20 Minuten Verspätung."* |
| Connection | **der Anschluss** | *"Erreichen wir den Anschlusszug?"* |
| Validate the ticket | **die Fahrkarte entwerter / abstempeln** | Crucial! Unstamped tickets lead to a €60 fine. |

## 🍽️ 4. Dining Out, Cafés & Restaurants

### At the Restaurant
| Phase | You Say / Waiter Says | Meaning / German Mindset |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Arrival** | *"Guten Abend, haben Sie einen Tisch für zwei Personen?"* | Do you have a table for two? |
| **Ordering Drinks** | *"Ich nehme ein stilles Wasser / ein Pils, bitte."* | Still water (*ohne Kohlensäure*) vs. sparkling (*mit Kohlensäure*). |
| **Ordering Food** | *"Ich hätte gerne das Schnitzel mit Kartoffelsalat."* | *Ich hätte gerne...* (I would like...) is the most polite formula. |
| **Dietary Needs** | *"Gibt es auch vegetarische / vegane Gerichte?"* | Are there vegetarian/vegan dishes? |
| **Allergies** | *"Ich bin allergisch gegen Nüsse / Gluten / Laktose."* | I am allergic to nuts/gluten/lactose. |
| **Enjoy your meal!** | **"Guten Appetit!"** | Answer: *"Danke, gleichfalls!"* |
| **Asking for the Bill** | *"Wir möchten bitte zahlen." / "Die Rechnung, bitte."* | Never wait for the bill automatically—you must ask. |
| **Split or Together?** | Waiter: **"Zusammen oder getrennt?"** | *Getrennt* (separately) is standard and welcomed in Germany! |
| **Tipping** | Add ~5% to 10%. If the total is €27.20, hand €30 and say: **"Machen Sie bitte 30 Euro."** | The waiter makes change directly at the table. |

## 🩺 6. Doctor, Pharmacy & Emergencies

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Emergency Numbers in Germany & the EU**:
> - **112**: Fire & Medical Emergency (*Feuerwehr & Rettungsdienst*).
> - **110**: Police (*Polizei*).
> - **116 117**: Non-emergency medical on-call doctor (*Ärztlicher Bereitschaftsdienst*).

### Emergency Scripts
| English | German | Urgency Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Help! | **Hilfe!** | Extreme emergency. |
| Call an ambulance! | **Rufen Sie einen Krankenwagen!** | Immediate medical danger. |
| Call the police! | **Rufen Sie die Polizei!** | Immediate security danger. |
| There has been an accident. | **Es gab einen Unfall.** | Provide location, number of injured people. |

### At the Doctor's Office (*Beim Arzt*) & Pharmacy (*In der Apotheke*)
| English | German | Diagnostic Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I feel sick / unwell. | **Mir ist schlecht / unwohl.** | Notice Dativ: *Mir ist...* (NOT *Ich bin schlecht*!). |
| I have a headache / stomachache. | **Ich habe starke Kopfschmerzen / Bauchschmerzen.** | Compound words: *Kopf* + *Schmerzen*. |
| I have a fever and a sore throat. | **Ich habe Fieber und Halsschmerzen.** | Typical cold symptoms. |
| I need a doctor's note for my employer. | **Ich brauche eine Arbeitsunfähigkeitsbescheinigung (AU / Krankschreibung).** | Essential German workplace paperwork. |
| Do I need a prescription for this? | **Ist dieses Medikament rezeptpflichtig?** | *Rezeptpflichtig* vs. *rezeptfrei* (over-the-counter). |
| How often should I take this? | **Wie oft soll ich die Tabletten einnehmen?** | *"Dreimal täglich vor dem Essen."* |
