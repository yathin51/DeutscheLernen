# A2 - Elementary (Grundlegende Kenntnisse)

Welcome to the A2 level resources. This folder contains materials for elementary German, building on the fundamentals established in A1.

## Master Grammar Guide
- 📖 [**A2 Grammatik-Leitfaden (Grammatik.md)**](./Grammatik.md) — Comprehensive pedagogical master guide on the Perfekt tense, Wechselpräpositionen (Wo vs. Wohin), Dative case mastery, Nebensätze (*weil, dass, wenn*), and coordinating conjunctions.

## Core Topics
- **Daily Routines & Work**: Talking about your job, daily activities, and professional background.
- **Travel & Directions**: Asking for directions, booking trips, and sharing travel experiences.
- **Expressing Past Events**: Narrating past experiences and activities.
- **Basic Grammar**:
  - Perfect tense (*Perfekt*) with *haben* and *sein*
  - Simple Past tense (*Präteritum*) for auxiliary and modal verbs (*war, hatte, musste...*)
  - Dative case (indirect objects, dative prepositions)
  - Comparative and superlative forms of adjectives
  - Basic coordinating conjunctions (*und, aber, oder, denn, sondern*)
