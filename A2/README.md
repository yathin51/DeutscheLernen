## 🇬🇧 English Speaker's Milestone Map for A2

Stepping up from A1 to A2 means moving from simple isolated sentences to **connected discourse and storytelling**. Here are the 3 big cognitive leaps for English speakers:

### 1. The Dative Case ("To Someone")
- In English: *"I give the waiter the tip"* (no preposition needed, the position reveals the indirect object).
- In German: the receiver gets marked by **Dative**: `der Kellner` becomes **`dem Kellner`** (*Ich gebe dem Kellner das Trinkgeld*).

### 2. Narrative Past: Spoken German uses Perfekt
- English speakers often mistakenly look for simple past (*ich ging, ich kaufte*).
- **Rule:** In German conversation, **always use Perfekt** (*ich bin gegangen, ich habe gekauft*). Save Präteritum only for *war* (was), *hatte* (had), and modal verbs (*musste, konnte*).

### 3. Subordinate Clauses: The Verb-Kicker Conjunctions
- In English: *"I stay home because I am sick"* (SVO preserved).
- In German: Conjunctions like *weil* (because), *dass* (that), and *wenn* (if/when) kick the verb to the end: *"Ich bleibe zu Hause, weil ich krank **bin**"*.
