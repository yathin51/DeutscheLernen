# A2 Grammar: Prepositions (Präpositionen)

At the A2 level, you learn which prepositions dictate either the **Accusative** or the **Dative** case, and how to handle **Two-Way Prepositions** (*Wechselpräpositionen*).

---

## 1. Accusative-Only Prepositions
These prepositions always require the noun phrase following them to be in the **Accusative** case:

- **bis** (until / up to): Der Zug fährt **bis** Berlin.
- **durch** (through): Wir gehen **durch den** Wald. (Masc. Acc: *den*)
- **für** (for): Das Geschenk ist **für meinen** Vater. (Masc. Acc: *meinen*)
- **gegen** (against / around time): Er fährt **gegen den** Baum. / Wir treffen uns **gegen** 18 Uhr.
- **ohne** (without): Er kommt **ohne seine** Freundin.
- **um** (around / at time): Wir gehen **um den** See. / Der Film beginnt **um** 20 Uhr.

*Mnemonic:* **FUDGE** (**F**ür, **U**m, **D**urch, **G**egen, **E**ntlang)

---

## 2. Dative-Only Prepositions
These prepositions always require the noun phrase following them to be in the **Dative** case:

- **aus** (out of / from): Er kommt **aus dem** Zimmer. (Neut. Dat: *dem*)
- **bei** (at / with): Ich bin **bei meinem** Freund. (Masc. Dat: *meinem*)
- **mit** (with / by vehicle): Ich fahre **mit dem** Auto. (Neut. Dat: *dem*)
- **nach** (after / to cities/countries): **Nach dem** Unterricht gehe ich nach Hause.
- **seit** (since / for duration): Ich wohne **seit einem** Monat in Hamburg.
- **von** (from / of): Das ist das Haus **von meiner** Tante. (Fem. Dat: *meiner*)
- **zu** (to local destinations/people): Ich gehe **zum** Arzt (*zu dem* -> *zum*).

*Mnemonic Song (tune of Blue Danube):*
*Aus, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu* - all take the Dative!

---

## 3. Two-Way Prepositions (Wechselpräpositionen)
These nine prepositions can take **either** the Accusative or the Dative case, depending on the question they answer.

| Preposition | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **an** | on (vertical, like a wall) / at |
| **auf** | on (horizontal, like a table) |
| **in** | in / into |
| **hinter** | behind |
| **neben** | next to / beside |
| **über** | over / above |
| **unter** | under / below |
| **vor** | in front of |
| **zwischen** | between |

### The Rule: Direction vs. Location

#### A. Accusative Case -> Answers **Wohin?** (Where to?)
Describes **movement, direction, or change of position** from one place to another.
- **Example**: Ich lege das Buch **auf den** Tisch. (I lay the book onto the table. - *Action of placing*)
- **Example**: Er geht **in das (ins)** Zimmer. (He walks into the room. - *Movement crossing a boundary*)

#### B. Dative Case -> Answers **Wo?** (Where?)
Describes **location, position, or state of rest** at a stationary spot.
- **Example**: Das Buch liegt **auf dem** Tisch. (The book lies on the table. - *Static location*)
- **Example**: Er ist **in dem (im)** Zimmer. (He is in the room. - *Static location*)
