# A2 Grammar: Pronouns (Pronomen)

At the A2 level, you learn how personal pronouns decline in both Accusative and Dative cases, and you are introduced to reflexive pronouns.

---

## 1. Personal Pronouns (Declined)

When replacing nouns with pronouns, make sure to use the correct case (Nominative, Accusative, or Dative).

| Nominative | Accusative | Dative | English Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ich** | mich | mir | I / me / to me |
| **du** | dich | dir | you / you / to you (informal) |
| **er** | ihn | ihm | he / him / to him |
| **sie** | sie | ihr | she / her / to her |
| **es** | es | ihm | it / it / to it |
| **wir** | uns | uns | we / us / to us |
| **ihr** | euch | euch | you all / you all / to you all |
| **sie** | sie | ihnen | they / them / to them |
| **Sie** | Sie | Ihnen | You / You / to You (formal) |

### Pronoun Word Order (A2 Rule):
If a sentence has both a noun object and a pronoun object, the **pronoun** always comes first.
- Ich gebe **meinem Bruder** (Dative noun) **das Buch** (Accusative noun).
- Ich gebe **es** (Accusative pronoun) **meinem Bruder** (Dative noun).
- Ich gebe **ihm** (Dative pronoun) **das Buch** (Accusative noun).

---

## 2. Reflexive Pronouns (Reflexivpronomen)

Reflexive pronouns are used with reflexive verbs (verbs where the subject performs the action on themselves, like *sich waschen* - to wash oneself).

In German, reflexive pronouns are either Accusative or Dative. They only differ from regular personal pronouns in the 3rd person singular and plural (**sich**).

| Subject | Accusative Reflexive | Dative Reflexive | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ich** | mich | mir | Ich dusche **mich**. / Ich putze **mir** die Zähne. |
| **du** | dich | dir | Du wäschst **dich**. / Du wäschst **dir** die Hände. |
| **er/sie/es** | **sich** | **sich** | Er verspätet **sich**. / Sie kämmt **sich** die Haare. |
| **wir** | uns | uns | Wir treffen **uns**. |
| **ihr** | euch | euch | Ihr freut **euch**. |
| **sie/Sie** | **sich** | **sich** | Sie interessieren **sich** für Musik. |
