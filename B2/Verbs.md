# B2 Grammar: Verbs (Verben)

At the B2 level, you learn advanced tenses to express assumptions and alternative structures to express passive concepts.

---

## 1. Future Perfect (Futur II)
*Futur II* is used to express:
1. An assumption or probability about an action that **will be completed in the future**.
2. An assumption or probability about an action that **took place in the past**.

$$\text{Subject} + \text{werden (conjugated)} + \dots + \text{Past Participle} + \text{haben/sein (infinitive)}$$

- **Example (Future completion)**: 
  - Bis morgen **werde** ich die Hausaufgaben **gemacht haben**. (By tomorrow I will have done the homework.)
- **Example (Past assumption)**: 
  - Er schläft nicht. Er **wird** gestern zu viel **gearbeitet haben**. (He is not sleeping. He must have worked too much yesterday.)
  - Sie ist nicht da. Sie **wird** nach Hause **gegangen sein**. (She is not there. She must have gone home.)

---

## 2. Alternative Passive Constructions (Passiversatz)
In academic or professional German, the standard passive (*werden* + *Partizip II*) is sometimes replaced by more elegant structures. These structures convey ability or necessity.

### A. *sein* + *zu* + Infinitive
Expresses necessity (must be done) or capability (can be done). It has a passive meaning but uses active syntax.
- **Passive**: Die Arbeit **muss gemacht werden**.
- **Alternative**: Die Arbeit **ist zu machen**. (The work is to be done / must be done.)
- **Passive**: Der Brief **kann nicht gelesen werden**.
- **Alternative**: Der Brief **ist nicht zu lesen**. (The letter cannot be read.)

### B. *sich lassen* + Infinitive
Expresses capability (can be done).
- **Passive**: Das Problem **kann gelöst werden**.
- **Alternative**: Das Problem **lässt sich lösen**. (The problem can be solved / lets itself be solved.)
- **Passive**: Die Tür **konnte nicht geöffnet werden**.
- **Alternative**: Die Tür **ließ sich nicht öffnen**. (The door could not be opened.)

### C. Adjectives ending in `-bar` or `-lich`
Many adjectives convey passive capability directly:
- *lösen* (to solve) -> **lösbar** (solvable / can be solved)
  - Das Problem ist **lösbar**.
- *trinken* (to drink) -> **trinkbar** (potable / drinkable)
  - Das Wasser ist **trinkbar**.
